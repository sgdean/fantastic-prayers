Inside Fantastic Prayers

Ten sections. Eight worlds.

The Opening Sequence and Credits frame eight principal interactive environments. Each follows its own logic of image, sound, motion, memory, and surprise.

Each page will pair a short recording of the original running in the with design notes, recovered material, and, where it makes sense, a small browser reassembly.

In brief

This is a first map. The descriptions will get richer as I work through the design document one section at a time and compare it with the original running in the emulator.

Opening Sequence

A timed sequence of images and sound that opens the work, then chooses one of its principal environments at random.

Jacket

Jacket unfolds through several linked subsections, including Fabric, Indigo, Label, Hair, Design, and Tortoise. It is a reminder that even one environment can contain a whole collection of smaller worlds.

Page started

Natatorium

A panoramic view of dense, graffitied walls inside an abandoned natatorium. The surface can be scratched, rolled over, clicked, and uncovered as layer after layer appears through small visual changes and a dense soundscape.

Page started

Empathy Wheel

As I remember it, this was the first environment we built. Its circular interface also gives the shared video-eye navigation tool a special behavior of its own.

Credits

Credits is more than an end screen. You can return to the previous environment with its earlier conditions intact, or quit the work.

Page in preparation

Short recordings from the emulator.

I plan to make a thirty-to-sixty-second screen recording from each principal environment so people can see and hear how it works.

These won't be tours or summaries. They will be glimpses, with just enough movement, sound, and interaction to show why we want to bring the work back to life. They also make all the invisible labor of getting the original to run useful to everyone who visits this site.

The original audio tour

The historical Dia site also offered a five-minute audio tour, with voices by Sara Tucker and Lena Dunham and guitar by Stephen Vitiello. It says the tour could be heard by playing the CD-ROM in an audio CD player. We still need to confirm whether that separate audio session survives in the preservation materials.
